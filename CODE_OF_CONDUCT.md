# Code of conduct

This project is a small kit for people who are searching for work, which is often a stressful time. The aim is a space where people can ask for help, share what went wrong and improve the kit without worrying about being judged.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. People here may be tired, between jobs or writing in a second language.
- Say what you mean plainly. Disagree with an idea, not with the person.
- Keep other people's data private. Never post a real tracker, CV, career file, recruiter message or employer name that is not yours to share.
- Welcome questions from beginners, designers and non-coders as much as from engineers.
- Accept that this is one person's project with limited time. Reviews and replies may be slow.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Posting someone else's personal data, or trying to identify people from the sample data.
- Sexual language or attention, threats, or sustained pressure after someone has said no.
- Using the project to scrape, spam or contact recruiters and employers in bulk.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, email ux@joselblas.com with a link or a description. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept private. If the report is about the maintainer, say so, and ask for a second opinion from another trusted contributor.

You will get a reply, usually within a week. Where it is useful, the response is a private message first, and a block only if the behaviour continues or is serious.
